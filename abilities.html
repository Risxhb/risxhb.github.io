Skill animations

Priest spell effects

A holy cast circle under the priest, then Lesser Heal, Smite and Barrier of Light, and what happens when a prayer is interrupted. All recordings are frames from the Windows player at half size.

Status: the spell effects were independently approved at 8.3/10 on the fourth review pass. The ability proof passes its automated checks (Editor 22/22, Windows player 20/20) but has not been played by a person.

The four effects

One 10.6 second showcase: Lesser Heal, Smite, Barrier of Light (struck once, then broken) and a prayer interrupted mid-cast. The capsules stand in for the priest and an ally.

EffectWhat is shown
Holy cast circleGold line work under the priest. The outer ring draws itself clockwise over the cast time, then flashes white and opens.
Interrupted prayerThe circle turns cold blue-white, cracks into dashes and slides inward. Gone in 0.45 s with no release flash.
Lesser HealA soft column of warm light settles on the ally, a gold ring lands at the feet and motes rise along the body.
SmiteA sun mark tightens under the enemy for the 0.7 s cast, then a narrow white-gold shaft comes down and leaves a scorch ring.
Barrier of LightA cage of pale light with six ribs. A hit makes it flinch; a break throws long shards that fall to the floor.

Driven by the ability rules

A small room with a Warrior, a Priest and one scavenger. The rules decide who can act, what it costs, when a prayer is interrupted and how much is healed or absorbed; the effects only show what the rules report. Both party members use the guild clerk as a stand-in body.
