What’s new in this demo

October 26, 2026

The Arcanist has been rebuilt as a ranged glass cannon, with five new spells, new animations and arcane effects. Its dash is now a blink. It has no defensive ability, so keep moving.

Arcanist abilities

  • Magic MissileLeft click

    Tap: one missile straight at the cursor (10). It can miss.

    Hold: summon up to 3 missiles, one every 0.25 s; release to fire them. They home on the boss (13 each).

    You move at half speed while charging. Dashing drops the charge.

  • Disintegrate2 · hold

    A beam you steer toward the cursor for up to 2 s. Damage builds in 3 steps the longer you hold.

    You move at 20% speed. Release to stop; dashing breaks the beam. Cooldown 8 s.

  • Rune Sigil3

    Draws a rune at the cursor (16 m). After 0.6 s it explodes (55) if the boss is inside it (2.5 m).

    Cooldown 6 s.

  • Astral Orb4

    Charge 0.75 s to release the astrolabe’s orb. For 8 s it fires a homing bolt every second (8 each) while you do anything else.

    Dashing during the charge cancels it for free. Cooldown 10 s from the cast.

  • Arcane Comet5

    Marks a spot at the cursor (20 m); the comet lands 2 s later (90, 4 m).

    The bullseye in the middle doubles it. The mark stays where you put it. Cooldown 18 s.

  • BlinkRight click

    The shared dash, shown as a teleport. Short burst (3 m in 0.2 s) in the move direction. Costs 30 stamina; sprint and jump share the bar.

    No invincibility: get out of the shape before it resolves. Cancels basic attacks and unreleased windups.

Plain numbers in brackets, like (10), are damage. Hover over an ability in game to see its tooltip.

Controls & about this demo

Choose your class above Start Run and select a difficulty, then start the run. A shuffled boss rush ends with Bellkeeper. Boss-Rush Test Rush lets you choose a map and encounter for practice.

Move with WASD, hold Shift to sprint and press Space to jump. Aim with the mouse. Left click for your basic attack (the Arcanist can hold it to charge missiles), 2–5 for class skills, right click to dash, F for a potion and Escape to pause.

Work in progress. Alternative Confrontation is the default main menu. Settings > UI can switch to the five-arena showcase. Boss music continues between fights and crossfades during each countdown. Browser performance may vary.
