Environment

Town

The town was grey and blue boxes on a lawn. It is now a walled market town: 191 buildings from a new building kit, a cathedral, the lord's garrison, gardens, washing lines, chimney smoke and lamp-lit doors. It is in the game.

Status: integrated into the live connected town on 14 October. The game's own walk through every area passes in the Editor (1,451 of 1,451 checks) and in a Windows player at two screen shapes (1,358 and 1,361 of the same), plus save-and-resume. Independent critics scored the steps between 5 and 7 out of 10; the final version is unscored. Nobody has walked it by hand yet, and townsfolk come later.

Signs of life

No people yet; markers stand where they will. Until then: crates, kegs and sacks against walls; wood piles and washing in the yards; gardens and shade trees; a village green with a well; the garrison barracks with its drill gear; smoke from the chimneys; and an evening look with warm windows and light under the door lanterns.

  • Cost: the town went from 368,000 to about 1.6 million triangles. The Windows player ran the whole walk; frame time has not been measured.
  • Changed from the earlier artist’s work: 73 small wall props on rebuilt houses are switched off (the new houses carry their own), four props that cut into walls were moved, and the coloured puddle and moss marks were recoloured.
  • Not done: townsfolk, sound, interiors for the new buildings.

EarlierThe cathedral

Plain stone, no symbols yet: a tall nave with aisles and buttresses, a great east window and a tower over the door with a slate spire about 36 m high. It stands at the end of the residential lane, so the tower closes the view up the street. Its door now leads inside: see the cathedral. The sanctuary nearby keeps its footprint with a new porch and a bell tower you can now see.

EarlierA building kit

One generator builds every house from a short description: plaster, half-timber or stone walls; tiled gable, hip and lean-to roofs; dormers, chimneys, shutters, window boxes, shopfronts with awnings, lanterns, back doors and outshots; terraces that share walls. The textures were drawn by script for this town, with damp at the foot of walls and soot on chimney tops.

EarlierFavilla, the goddess of the square (18 October)

The old block figure on the fountain is gone. Favilla now stands on a new carved fountain: white marble, with honey-coloured marble at her sleeve ends and a little worn gilding. Her right hand is held out over the plaza in blessing; her left holds a carved flame that glows softly at dusk (no light is added). She is turned slightly toward the gate.

She went through eight whole-statue critic rounds and a sculpt pass. The last critic scored her 5.8 of 8: she reads well from the gate and across the square, but close up the face, hair and robes still need a sculptor, so she is in the game as provisional. The game's walk through the town passes except one check that already failed before she was added (a companion catching on a market stall).

Favilla takes Eve's face (19 October)

The statue now has the same face as Eve, the base every character is built from, carved as one solid stone head with the eyes cut in. She looks down toward the plaza with her lids slightly lowered, lips gently pressed and a slight smile. The rough patches the old carved face left in the marble are gone. Critics scored her 6.0 of 8 overall and the face 6.5 (up from 5.5); she is still provisional until a sculptor pass. The game's full walk through every area passes with her in place (1,538 of 1,538 checks).
