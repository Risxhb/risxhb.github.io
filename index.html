Work log · 13–15 October 2026

Ashen Covenant

A party dungeon game in Unity. Newest work first: the main menu, Serelle the priest companion, the first undead enemy, starting gear, ragdolls and the dialogue box. Earlier work is folded below.

Latest

  1. 18 OctBlood Mage spellsFive spells that rise out of a circle of the mage's own blood: a lance, a drain torn from the enemy, a ticking bleed, hands that grip the legs and a shell of blood glass. Each had four critic passes and is accepted for now at 7.4 to 7.7. Not in the game yet.
  2. 18 OctFavilla in the townThe marble goddess Favilla now stands on a new carved fountain in the town square, blessing the plaza, in place of the old block figure. Her carved flame glows at dusk. Installed as provisional (critics 5.8 of 8); a sculptor pass on the face, hair and robes comes later.
Earlier17 Oct · 3 items
  1. 17 OctSpell effects v002Smite and the whole priest kit redrawn as flowing light that burns away with a hot edge. Smite v002 lands on the floor under the target and stops under ceilings. Both approved by independent critics (Smite 8.1, the kit 8.1 after four passes) and now in the game in place of v001; the main game build gets them at its next rebuild.
  2. 17 OctWarrior movesThe Warrior's strike, shield bash, taunt, guard, dodges, stagger and six talent moves on the male adventurer, with their effects reworked to follow his blade and shield. Waiting for your review.
  3. 17 OctSerelle's spellsSerelle now casts: her own movement for each of the ten Priest spells, in time with the spell effects, plus a prayer book that floats open with turning pages for Goddess' Mercy and Benediction. She is also the player's body when you choose the Priest. In a test build; waiting for your review (critics 4.0 to 5.7 of your 7).
Earlier15 Oct · 7 items
  1. 15 OctMale adventurerThe male adventurer is the player when you choose the Warrior: a sword, a round shield with the Covenant's flame, a scabbard and 15 combat moves. In a test build of the game; the main game gets him at its next rebuild. Waiting for your review.
  2. 15 OctMain menuThe game has a 3D main menu, built from your concept: one iron lantern on an old dungeon wall, a gilt title it reveals when the flame swells, a real candle flame, worn oak and bronze. Refined over five passes overnight.
  3. 15 OctDialogue boxThe dialogue box is wired into the game: walk up, press E, read, choose. First use is narration at the guild memorial. Waiting for your review.
  4. 15 OctRagdollDefeated characters now fall as ragdolls: the body goes limp, is thrown away from whoever struck it, and lies still once it comes to rest; a revive stands it back up. Tested on Serelle and Enosh (49 of 49 checks); in the main game at its next rebuild.
  5. 15 OctSerelleSerelle, the priest companion, is in the game: the first character built from Eve, after your reference, in red vestments. She takes the companion's place whenever the companion is the Priest, and is in the model viewer.
  6. 15 OctRisenThe first undead enemy, built from Enosh: skull under skin, a cowl, torn quilting and a rusted sword, with its own idle, walk, run, slash, hit and death. Work in progress (critics 5.4 of 8); not in the game.
  7. 15 OctStarting gearWarrior and Priest starter kits for both Eve and Enosh: you chose Warrior C and Priest A; body hiding under armour and hair under hoods work. Stopped after SG4 for you.
