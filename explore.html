Scene viewer

Areas

The saved Unity scenes, exported as they stood today. Pick an area and move through it.

What this is: the scene geometry, textures and lamp positions, lit by the browser. It is not the game: shaders, shadows, water, effects, the camera cut-away and collisions are not here, so you can pass through walls and the light is an approximation.
