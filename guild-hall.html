Adventurers' guild hall

Seven layout candidates in one day, v002 to v008, each a copy of the one before in its own scene: floors and walls, a service counter, modelled furniture, the muster yard and the memorial wall.

Status: these are candidate scenes. The guild in the connected game is unchanged. The counter, furniture and yard were accepted step by step; the memorial reached 7.4/10 from an independent critic against a target of 8 and is not approved. No Play Mode or player build was run on the candidates.

The hall from above

Service counter and hall floor

The reception boxes became an 8.5 m counter with three service bays, a staff flap and a dais for Iroha. 49 blockout boxes were replaced by 22 modelled pieces: a rank board, trestle tables, benches, supply racks and a hearth.

The muster yard

The courtyard got a stone and lime-washed wall with tiled coping, an arched gate, a covered walk, a count post with a muffled bell and a drill corner.

The memorial wall

Rebuilt over four runs, each scored by a separate critic from its captures: 5.5, 6.4, 7.0, 7.4. A stepped plinth, an arched niche with the kept flame, bronze plates and empty places, tallow candles and things left on the ledge.
