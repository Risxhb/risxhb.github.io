Adventurers' guild hall

Ten layout candidates in one day, v002 to v011, each a copy of the one before in its own scene: floors and walls, a service counter, modelled furniture, the muster yard, the memorial, a second storey, a roof and lamps.

Status: these are candidate scenes. The guild in the connected game is unchanged and nothing is integrated yet. The counter, furniture and yard were accepted step by step; the stair, gallery, upper storey, roof and lighting wait for review; the memorial reached 7.4/10 from an independent critic against a target of 8 and is not approved. The final candidate passed its static checks with zero failures and was walked by a stand-in in an isolated player, not by the game's own walker.

The hall from above

Service counter and hall floor

The reception boxes became an 8.5 m counter with three service bays, a staff flap and a dais for Iroha. 49 blockout boxes were replaced by 22 modelled pieces: a rank board, trestle tables, benches, supply racks and a hearth. In the final candidate Iroha is her newest model, v053.

The muster yard

The courtyard got a stone and lime-washed wall with tiled coping, an arched gate, a covered walk, a count post with a muffled bell and a drill corner. The brown slabs that marked the dungeon doors became thresholds let into the floor.

The memorial wall

Rebuilt over four runs, each scored by a separate critic from its captures: 5.5, 6.4, 7.0, 7.4. A stepped plinth, an arched niche with the kept flame, bronze plates and empty places, tallow candles and things left on the ledge.

Stair to the east wall, memorial to the gallery

At your direction the stair now runs flush against the east wall where the memorial stood, and the memorial stands on the gallery facing the hall. Its design is unchanged; it is out from under the deck and can be seen across the room.

Upper storey, roof and light

A second storey on all four sides, merged into the wall objects so the camera hides lower and upper together. A pitched tiled roof with gable ends: one object that the camera hides whole while you are inside and shows when you are out. Lamps over the board and under the gallery, a fire in the hearth, and eight new lights; the flat ambient light came down so they can be seen.

Walked in an isolated player

A small player holds only the final candidate, with a prototype camera that zooms and turns in quarter steps. A blue stand-in walked to the counter, along the boards, under the gallery, through the yard to the gate and up the stair, and the player photographed itself. It is not the game: the game's camera and walker are untouched, and whether the game adopts this camera is not decided.

What the final candidate was checked for

  • 261 of 261 prop placements match the table; 21 places on the ground floor can be reached from the entrance and back; four points on the gallery have the deck under them and room to stand.
  • Iroha's Idle animation was sampled at nine poses: nothing passes through the counter, the dais, the frame behind her or the wall.
  • No blockout box is drawn any more, and the floating study labels are gone.
  • Not checked: the game's own walker and camera, play by hand, performance. Nothing is in the game until it is integrated; a list of every change is written for that.
