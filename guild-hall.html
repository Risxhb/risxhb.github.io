The memorial, fifth pass

One more pass, as you allowed: the oath carved as letters and words, boots with a toe, heel and shaft, a pale ash band, duller plates, the niche lit by its flame, the tag, helm and sword reworked. A separate critic scored it 7.7 (up from 7.4) against your 8. What still holds it back: the ash reads as a sparkle effect, from the default camera it is still a grey slab, and the boots sit behind a rail that looks like crowd barriers.

EarlierStair to the east wall, memorial to the gallery

At your direction the stair now runs flush against the east wall where the memorial stood, and the memorial stands on the gallery facing the hall. Its design is unchanged; it is out from under the deck and can be seen across the room.

EarlierThe memorial wall

Rebuilt over four runs, each scored by a separate critic from its captures: 5.5, 6.4, 7.0, 7.4. A stepped plinth, an arched niche with the kept flame, bronze plates and empty places, tallow candles and things left on the ledge.

EarlierThe muster yard

The courtyard got a stone and lime-washed wall with tiled coping, an arched gate, a covered walk, a count post with a muffled bell and a drill corner. The brown slabs that marked the dungeon doors became thresholds let into the floor.

EarlierService counter and hall floor

The reception boxes became an 8.5 m counter with three service bays, a staff flap and a dais for Iroha. 49 blockout boxes were replaced by 22 modelled pieces: a rank board, trestle tables, benches, supply racks and a hearth. In the final candidate Iroha is her newest model, v053.
